The dignified ledger

Money between people, handled with care

Informal lending runs on trust — and trust runs on memory. Ụgwọ remembers every kobo for you, so awkward conversations never have to happen.

⚡

Log in under 10 seconds

Who, how much, when, and an optional due date. One floating button, one short form, done.

🤝

People, not transactions

Your home screen is a list of people and where you stand with each — one clean net balance per person.

💌

Face-saving reminders

"Hi Tobi, gentle reminder about the outstanding ₦70,000 from 14 June. Thank you 🙏" — composed for you, shared your way.

🔔

Reminders that never leak

Due-date alerts at 7, 3 and 1 day out — scheduled privately on your phone. Our servers can't even see the dates.

📄

PDF statements

Export a beautiful statement of every debt and repayment — generated entirely on your device.

🎉

One small celebration

No streaks, no gamification — just one quiet moment of joy every time a debt is fully settled.

We can't see your debts. Nobody can.

Debts are personal. Ụgwọ encrypts every record on your phone before it ever leaves — names, amounts, dates, notes. What reaches our servers is unreadable ciphertext.

End-to-end encryption

AES-256-GCM with a key only your devices hold. Our servers sync ciphertext they cannot read.

Device-level lock

Face ID, fingerprint, or your phone's PIN protect the app. Nothing extra to remember.

Works offline

Your ledger lives on your phone first. No signal, no problem — sync catches up when you're back.

Your data, your call

Export everything as a PDF, or delete your account and every record in one tap.
